# Preregistered research rules

This file is fixed before calculating strategy returns. All timestamps are UTC. Research is limited to the data named in TASK.md. Use 2016-10-01 through 2021-12-31 for development (2019-08-01 start for US500/DE30 ideas); use 2022-01-01 through 2026-09-30 as a single holdout. No parameter search or alteration after seeing returns. A trading date must have all required quote timestamps. At most one trade per idea per date.

Signals use bid/ask midpoints at the close of the stated minute. Entry and scheduled exit use the first bar **open** at or after the stated minute, requiring a quote within two minutes. Buy at ask, sell at bid. A stop is a fixed distance from entry's midprice, checked against subsequent bid lows for longs and ask highs for shorts; if a bar crosses the stop, fill at the stop or the bar's adverse open, whichever is worse. Stop wins any same-bar tie. No target orders. A scheduled exit uses the 17:00, 12:00, 11:00 or 09:00 open as specified below. If missing, close on the first subsequent open within 15 minutes; otherwise drop the date before signal evaluation. No trades cross a day boundary, so overnight swap is zero. Commissions are assumed included in the retail bid/ask spread; this omits any extra account-specific charges.

Use these six distinct hypotheses and fixed thresholds. A zero signal creates no trade. Signal prices require a bar within two minutes of the stated timestamp. The signal unit is midpoint log return, with thresholds in basis points (bp; 1 bp = 0.0001 log return). Require Monday-Friday entry and exit.

1. **US equities lead yen:** US500 14:30 to 15:00 return at least +15 bp or at most -15 bp; trade USDJPY in the same direction from 15:01 to 17:00. US equity risk appetite can alter dollar/yen hedging after index moves. Stop: 25 JPY pips.
2. **Gold leads Australian dollar:** XAUUSD 12:00 to 13:00 return at least +20 bp or at most -20 bp; trade AUDUSD in the same direction from 13:01 to 17:00. Gold and AUD can share commodity/risk demand. Stop: 20 FX pips.
3. **US close leads Germany:** US500 18:00 to 20:00 return at least +25 bp or at most -25 bp; trade DE30 in the same direction on the next calendar weekday from 07:01 to 09:00. Global equity information can transfer across closed cash markets. Stop: 100 DE30 index points. Skip any date lacking one of the required bars.
4. **Triangular FX delayed adjustment:** At 09:00, compute the 08:00 to 09:00 log return of EURUSD plus USDJPY minus EURJPY. If the residual is at least +5 bp, buy EURJPY; if at most -5 bp, sell EURJPY, 09:01 to 11:00. Cross rates may temporarily adjust slower than liquid USD legs. Stop: 25 JPY pips.
5. **Yen post-shock reversal:** If USDJPY's 13:00 to 14:00 return has magnitude at least 35 bp, trade against that move 14:01 to 17:00. Price pressure after an unusually large hourly move can reverse. Stop: 25 JPY pips.
6. **Quiet-day FX continuation:** Compute the preceding complete UTC weekday's EURUSD high-low midpoint range using 08:00-17:00 minute bars. It must be less than the median of the preceding 20 available weekday ranges (requiring all 20), and the current day's EURUSD 08:00 to 09:00 return must have magnitude at least 8 bp. Trade EURUSD in that return's direction 09:01 to 12:00. A quiet prior day may leave information to be incorporated during the next European session. Stop: 20 FX pips.

For each trade, report signed net quote difference / pip size, with point = 1 for DE30. A stopped trade includes the full realized spread. EUR 1,000 accounts trade each idea independently, sizing each trade so the quoted stop distance plus entry spread risks 2% of current equity. On each trade multiply equity by `1 + 0.02 * net_pips / (stop_pips + entry_spread_pips)`; allow equity to vary without caps, withdrawals, or transaction fees beyond spread. Drawdown is peak-to-trough of the trade-by-trade equity path including initial EUR 1,000. No overlapping positions within each idea.

For each idea, use a matched random-direction benchmark: same entry dates, instrument, exits, stops, and position sizing, replacing signal direction with an independent fair coin from NumPy seed 20261019. Its purpose is to show performance from the times alone. Report sample t-statistic of mean net pips versus zero and a two-sided Student t p-value. A holdout passes only if its mean pips is positive and its p-value is below 0.05/6 = 0.00833 (Bonferroni), and it exceeds the matched random benchmark mean. No signal is declared proven merely by full-sample or recent-period performance.
