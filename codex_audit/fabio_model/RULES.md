# Prespecified mechanical interpretation

Written before running `research.py`. Source: `transcript_model_chapters.txt`, especially 6:35–20:33 and 35:48–43:14. Fabio describes *models*, not fixed algorithms. In particular he says the aggression decision cannot simply be automated. All thresholds below are researcher choices, fixed here before looking at returns.

## Data, clock, and execution

- One-minute UTC CFD quotes. US indices use HistData bid bars before 2019-08-05, with ask = bid plus the median Exness **absolute** ask-minus-bid spread for that New York minute of day from 2019-08 through 2020-07. No synthetic tick counts are invented. Subsequent bars use Exness bid/ask. DAX and gold use Exness only, so their histories start in 2019. Gold's 2015 file is not used: it does not fill the 2018–19 gap and the requested reporting starts 2016-10.
- Convert UTC with IANA `America/New_York` and `Europe/London` (including both DST switches). NY trend scan is 09:30–15:30 NY time; London reversion scan is 08:00–11:30 London time. A trade exits on the last *available* bar at or before 15:59 NY (trend), or 12:00 London (reversion), with a 30-minute maximum hold for the base scalp. Never assume a 16:00 NY quote. No overnight trade.
- Build a prior-day reference from the previous trading day's 09:30–15:59 NY bars, using each minute's mid close as one time-at-price observation (TPO), even when `n` exists. Price bins have width 0.05% of that day's median price. POC is the most populated bin centre (lower price wins ties). Starting at POC, expand to the more populated adjacent bin (lower wins ties) until at least 70% of observations are included. The extreme included bin centres, expanded by half a bin, give VAL and VAH. Require at least 180 observations; no reference otherwise. This is **TPO, not exchange volume**.
- A leg profile uses the same bin width and the mid closes between confirmed swing A and B. LVN is the interior bin with the fewest TPO observations, requiring at least one observation and at most 70% of the leg's POC count. If tied choose the one nearest B. Its tradable zone is its bin's full width. Require at least five occupied bins. B and its profile are frozen before an entry is considered.
- Signal is read at the close of minute `t`; enter at the open of the next *available consecutive* minute (`t+1` exactly one minute later). Buy at ask and sell at bid. Exit a long at bid and a short at ask. If stop and target can both be reached during a minute, the stop wins. Gap through a stop exits at the worse opening quote; gap beyond a target exits at the target (no positive price improvement). Stops cannot be placed on the wrong side of the entry; reject that signal. Nominal tick for the 1–2 tick stop rule is 0.01 price units on these CFD feeds. The stop is one tick beyond the signal minute's adverse bid/ask extreme. Risk R is the entry-to-stop distance, including entry spread. Moving stop to break-even means the executable exit quote equals entry price.
- One position per model per day; ignore overlapping later signals. A missing quote gap ends an open trade at the last available closing quote. Stops and targets apply from the entry minute. No financing, commission, tax, slippage beyond quote spread, or price improvement is assumed; this can overstate live returns.

## Trend model

1. At the NY scan, establish direction when a mid close first leaves yesterday's value area: above VAH is long, below VAL is short. That is **out of balance**. Keep the first direction for that day.
2. A is the opposite extreme of mid closes in the preceding 60 minutes, including the breakout minute. B is the most favourable close after breakout. Confirm B only after a retracement of at least 25% of the A–B distance, with a minimum A–B distance of four profile bins. Build the A-to-B leg profile using data through B only.
3. On the first later bar that overlaps the LVN zone and closes in the trade direction (above its open for long, below for short), signal. The next bar's executable open is the entry. Stop is beyond the signal bar's adverse extreme. Set target to the nearest POC of one of the last ten *completed* NY profiles that is beyond the entry in the trade direction; if no such POC exists, skip. This makes the transcript's “previous balance area” target actionable for an outward trend. It is an interpretation: the transcript does not uniquely identify which earlier balance area to use.
4. After an intrabar favourable move of one initial R, move the stop to executable break-even starting with the **next** bar. Close all at target, stop or session/30-minute exit.

## Mean reversion model

1. In the London scan, yesterday's VA is the balance. Wait for the first close outside it, then a close back inside. Never enter that first re-entry. Direction points from the excursion back toward yesterday's POC. Require the POC to be ahead of the eventual entry.
2. A is the most extreme close in the outside excursion. From re-entry, B is the furthest favourable close; confirm it only after a retracement of at least 25% of A–B, after B has covered at least four bins and at least 20% of yesterday's VA width. This is a mechanical “second swing.” Freeze the A-to-B profile and locate its LVN.
3. First subsequent LVN overlap and directional close is the signal. Enter next bar. Stop and break-even rules match the trend model. Target is yesterday's POC. Exit at target, stop or session/30-minute exit.

## Variants and selection protocol

- Each model runs with **no aggression trigger** and with a proxy: signal minute `n` at or above the 90th percentile of the preceding 60 available minutes, plus `(up-dn)/(up+dn)` at least +0.2 for long or at most −0.2 for short. At least 30 prior minutes with tick data are required. HistData signals cannot pass. Quote tick frequency and up/down quote changes are **not** big executed orders, footprint, exchange delta or CVD; the proxy may miss the entire edge Fabio describes.
- Candidate improvements are: trade only in the first 90 minutes of the model's scan; require a minimum previous-day VA width of 0.5% of POC; target at 1R rather than POC; and extend the maximum hold from 30 to 60 minutes. These are tested individually on 2016-10-01 through 2021-12-31. The selected single improvement, if any, maximises NASDAQ training mean R subject to at least 30 trades. Selection is frozen before calculating 2022-01-01 through 2026-09-30. The base and all candidates are still disclosed in the report, including failures.
- For each actual entry, a seeded random-direction control flips the direction with 50% probability, uses the same time and initial stop *distance*, and uses a 1R target; it is compared with the real trade at 1R as an entry-direction diagnostic. Seed 20260921. This is not a formal null for a POC target.
- Per-trade t = mean R / (sample standard deviation / sqrt(n)); no serial-correlation correction. Two-sided Student-t p values get Bonferroni correction across all disclosed rule variants per market. No significance claim is made from the overlapping 10-year, 2-year and 2026 windows. Compound a EUR 1,000 account as `balance *= (1 + fraction * R)` for risk fractions 0.005 and 0.02; max drawdown is peak-to-trough on the trade equity curve, including initial EUR 1,000. No account floor or lot rounding is imposed.
