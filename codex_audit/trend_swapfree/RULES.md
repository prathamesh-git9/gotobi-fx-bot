# Pre-declared rules (written before calculating any returns)

**Main test: A12.** USD-notional proxy portfolio of EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, XAUUSD, US500, USTEC, DE30 and UK100. Daily signal/prices from Yahoo closes: `EURUSD=X`, `GBPUSD=X`, `USDJPY=X`, `GLD`, `^GSPC`, `QQQ`, `^GDAXI`, `^FTSE`. GLD and QQQ are ETF price proxies; `^GSPC` and `^FTSE` are price indices; `^GDAXI` is a DAX performance index. These are imperfect CFD proxies. Do not substitute `SPY` for `^GSPC` after seeing results. Current Exness swap-free eligibility covers all eight.

**Pre-declared variant B12:** add AUDUSD, NZDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, XAGUSD, using Yahoo's corresponding FX series and `SLV`. This is a broader eligible-universe test under today's Exness list, not a financing loophole. No other variants.

Signal at each Friday close (or last available close of the week): the arithmetic mean of the signs of trailing **21-, 63-, and 252-observation** price returns, each in {-1,0,+1}. If fewer than 252 observations for an instrument, exclude it until seasoned. Annualized volatility is the preceding **63 daily log-return observations**, multiplied by sqrt(252), floored at 5%. The signal and volatility are determined only from prices available through that close. Hold resulting weights from the next available trading-day close-to-close return through the next rebalance. No intraday stop or discretionary filter.

Each active instrument's target USD notional as a fraction of equity is `signal * 0.12/(sqrt(N)*volatility)`, with N the number of eligible seasoned instruments at the decision time. Thus equal ex-ante standalone risk before correlation, approximately 12% portfolio volatility if independent. Scale *all* notionals proportionally if gross leverage exceeds **2.0 times equity**. Cap each single instrument at **0.75 times equity**, then proportionally reapply the gross cap. Rebalance every week at the decision close; between decisions the number of proxy units stays fixed, so weights drift with price and equity. No parameter tuning on hold-out results.

**Prices/FX conversion.** Raw Yahoo close-to-close percentage changes proxy CFD price P&L. GBPUSD, EURUSD etc are USD quotes; for USDJPY the percentage return of USDJPY is the USD value return of a long USD/short JPY currency position to first order. Equity-index non-USD currency translation and CFD dividend adjustments are **not modeled**. GLD/SLV ETF and QQQ distributions/fees cause additional proxy mismatch. Exness minute midpoint daily closes will be used only for independent *signal direction agreement* checks where available, not spliced into the long Yahoo return history.

**Execution and financing.** Charge spread on every absolute unit change: `abs(new_units - old_units) * price * (annual instrument-year median Exness bid/ask spread / contemporaneous midprice)`. Use the conservative **full spread per one-way trade**, covering slippage and timing beyond the half-spread quoted-price crossing. Minute quotes are measured in the liquid overlapping hours 12:00–16:00 UTC, one file at a time; median across sampled minutes within each year. Use earliest measured year when earlier history has no Exness minute quotes and latest measured year if any late year lacks them. For variant B FX/silver without minute files, use contemporaneous Exness published Standard typical spreads, or explicitly stated conservative fixed proxies if no public value. No commission on Standard.

Two scenarios: (1) zero swap while status remains qualified; (2) lost status, charge **8% annualized of gross notional exposure every calendar day** (daily 8%/252 of daily gross exposure on trading days). This is an adverse flat estimate, not a reconstructed Exness rate; it is deliberately applied to both long and short because actual historical instrument/direction rates are unavailable. It captures a plausible broker funding markup on top of overnight financing, but may understate or overstate actual fees. No positive swap credit is assumed. Cash earns **zero** (EUR account proxy) in both scenarios. Price-only long benchmark holds the same instruments with equal initial capital weights, rebalanced weekly to equal weights, no leverage, same spread and financing cases. Cash benchmark is EUR 1,000 flat. Report dates use available trading days between the stated endpoints, annualized geometric return using 252 trading days and Sharpe with zero risk-free rate. Worst year is the minimum calendar-year return within the tested window (including partial years, labelled).

**Evaluation.** Design period ends 2015-12-31; hold-out is 2016-01-01 through 2026-09-30, examined once after these rules. Also present full feasible history, the three requested EUR 1,000 windows, benchmark comparison, per-year spread table, Exness/Yahoo signal agreement, and minimum-lot replication check. The portfolio is a **continuous-notional idealization**; executable lot rounding is a separate feasibility gate and cannot be concealed inside return estimates. Verdict requires both positive hold-out evidence and feasible sizing at EUR 1,000 under actual account terms.
