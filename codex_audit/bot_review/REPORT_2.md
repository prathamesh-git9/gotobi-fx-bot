## Newly exposed issues, ranked

### CRITICAL

1. **A pending `PLACED` order can produce two positions.** `bot/broker.py:36-37,166-174`; `bot/engine.py:496-503,602-622`. When the first order is queued but has no position/deal after two seconds, the bot sends another. The broker may then fill both. `test_placed_order_can_be_resent_while_still_pending` reproduced two positions and two entry deals. **Fix:** persist a request intent before sending, retain the returned order ID, inspect `orders_get` and order/deal history until the original is definitively canceled/rejected/filled, and never resend solely because two seconds passed. Cover process restart and partially filled orders.

2. **A completed fill followed by a position-read error can be duplicated.** `bot/engine.py:412-415,478-480,624-629`. The history guard is marked checked before `order_send`; if `_find_ticket` then raises, no slot is journaled. If the fill stops before the next poll, the next entry skips history and sends again. `test_fill_then_position_read_error_and_stop_can_duplicate` reproduced two entry deals. **Fix:** record durable intent before `order_send`; on any post-send exception, mark outcome unknown and recheck orders/deals before retrying. Repeat history reconciliation until a definitive slot outcome is recorded.

### HIGH

3. **Recovery can silently clear the kill switch.** `bot/journal.py:57-77,80-82,149-152`; `bot/engine.py:117-129,354-368`. The backup is taken only at startup. If the journal later halts and `state.json` corrupts, the backup predates the halt. Startup accepts it and can place a new trade. `test_corrupt_recovery_can_lose_kill_switch` did exactly that. **Fix:** make recovery fail closed for new entries until halt/peak/recent-R history has been independently reconciled, and preserve a current durable backup or replayable event log.

4. **A modest PC clock error passes the freshness guard and moves the fix trade.** `bot/clock.py:85-112`; `bot/engine.py:239-258,535-571,664-680`. With UDP 123 blocked and an Exness-like UTC+0 feed, a PC clock 60 seconds fast makes the physical 00:53:59 look like the intended 00:54:59. `test_blocked_time_servers_allow_short_a_minute_early` sent a short 61 seconds before the fix, while `sent_vs_fix_sec` said -1; `test_preflight_says_ready_with_unverified_clock_one_minute_ahead` returned READY. **Fix:** require recent independent UTC verification for the second-sensitive short, and make preflight fail its clock check when unavailable. For the long, define a much smaller maximum unchecked error or disable entries while clock accuracy is unknown.

5. **Structurally corrupt but valid JSON still aborts startup.** `bot/journal.py:49-63,37-42`; `bot/main.py:133-137`. `{"slots": []}` parses and is backed up as good, then `.items()` raises outside the startup `OSError` handler. `test_malformed_but_valid_json_crashes_journal_startup` reproduced this. The launcher will repeatedly restart without managing an open position. **Fix:** validate the complete journal schema before accepting or backing up a snapshot; quarantine invalid structure and recover fail closed.

### MEDIUM

6. **A partial fill is still recorded as a completed entry.** `bot/broker.py:34,161-162`; `bot/engine.py:478-484,577-586`. `test_partial_fill_is_still_classified_as_complete` showed `DONE_PARTIAL` returned as `done`. [MetaQuotes defines it as only part completed](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/constants/errorswarnings/enum_trade_return_codes); [RETURN filling can leave the remainder active](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/constants/tradingconstants/orderproperties) where that mode is allowed. The journal tracks only the immediate volume/ticket and may later ignore or duplicate the remainder. **Fix:** reconcile actual deals and remaining order volume through terminal order state before calling the entry complete.

7. **History errors eventually remove the closed trade from risk accounting.** `bot/engine.py:308-325,354-368`. After 131 failed history polls, `test_history_error_eventually_erases_closed_trade_performance` left `r=None` and `pnl=None`, so the 60-long rule cannot count the loss. **Fix:** keep the slot unresolved, block new entries while risk accounting is incomplete, and alert after bounded retries without converting a read error into an empty history.

The old short-deadline, sizing, minimum-lot, and state-growth defects remain in the 17-finding table. The original deadline characterization test still passes; a fresh pre-send timestamp cannot bound the subsequent synchronous terminal call or establish broker fill time.
