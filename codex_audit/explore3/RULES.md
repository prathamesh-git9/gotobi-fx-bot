# Preregistered scheduled-flow tests

Written before calculating any trade returns. Data are the permitted bid/ask minute bars only. All clocks below use the named local time zone, converted with daylight-saving rules. A date is eligible through 2026-09-30. Each idea is one hypothesis; seven tests receive Bonferroni correction.

## Common execution and validation

At the specified minute enter at the **ask open** for a long and **bid open** for a short. Exit at the bid/ask open of the minute `H` minutes later, or at the first stop/target touch during bars from entry minute through the minute before that exit. Long stops/targets use bid lows/highs; short stops/targets use ask highs/lows. If both touch in one bar, count the stop. Gap-through stops are filled at the worse of the stop and executable bar open; targets at the target level, never a better price. Require the entry, exit, and every intervening minute bar; skip missing intervals. No trade spans more than two hours. Fixed stop distance equals target distance: 25 pips for FX, 30 index points for US500, 100 index points for DE30. Pip = 0.01 for JPY and 0.0001 for other FX. Gold is not tested. A signal uses midpoints of the prior 30 minutes, with the prior-minute close compared with the 30-minute-prior open; zero means no trade.

The sole tuned parameter is H in {30, 60, 120} minutes. Select it by highest mean **event minus paired control** net return in stop units over 2016-2021 (2019-2021 for indices), with shorter H breaking ties. Select once, then evaluate 2022-01-01 through 2026-09-30. This selection is per idea. No other change after the holdout is viewed.

For each event, control is the same instrument, local weekday, and local entry clock on the same weekday **one week earlier**. Skip a pair if control is itself an event for that idea, either trade has missing bars, or either signal is zero. Compute each control's direction from its own preceding 30 minutes for momentum/reversal rules; fixed-direction controls use the same direction. The matched difference is event net result minus control net result in stop units. Use a one-sided paired permutation sign-flip test for mean difference > 0 (100,000 reproducibly seeded flips), and Bonferroni-adjusted p = min(1, 7p). A survivor needs adjusted p < 0.05, positive event mean, and positive event mean in both 2022-2024 and 2025-2026. Ordinary controls are only excluded when they satisfy the same event definition; a date can have a different scheduled event. Report every test, including failures. The p-value is a test against the paired ordinary clock, not a proof of a standalone profitable strategy.

For any survivor, report yearly net pips (index points for an index) from the full available sample. Compounding: begin with EUR 1,000, stake 2% of current equity per trade at the fixed stop distance, apply realized net stop-unit return, no leverage/margin ceiling, no currency conversion, and report separate runs starting at first available sample and 2025-01-01. This is a normalized risk illustration, not an executable EUR P&L estimate.

## Seven hypotheses

1. **US holiday reopening, US500.** First US weekday after a US federal holiday, excluding weekends and where the previous weekday was a holiday. At 09:30 New York, follow the preceding 30-minute mid move. Backlogged domestic orders after a cash-market closure may extend the early move.
2. **German holiday reopening, DE30.** First German weekday after a German national holiday, excluding weekends and where the previous weekday was a holiday. At 09:00 Berlin, follow the preceding 30-minute mid move. Deferred German cash equity orders may keep moving prices after reopening.
3. **UK holiday reopening, GBPUSD.** First UK weekday after a UK bank holiday, excluding weekends and where the previous weekday was a holiday. At 08:00 London, follow the preceding 30-minute mid move. Local corporate FX orders deferred over the closure may extend the opening move.
4. **Japan holiday reopening, USDJPY.** First Japanese weekday after a Japanese national holiday, excluding weekends and where the previous weekday was a holiday. At 09:00 Tokyo, follow the preceding 30-minute mid move. Deferred yen hedging and settlement flow may extend the Tokyo opening move.
5. **Quarterly FX futures expiry proxy, EURUSD.** Third Wednesday of March, June, September and December at 10:00 New York. Fade the prior 30-minute mid move. Expiring currency-futures hedges may unwind after the scheduled quarterly IMM window. This calendar is an approximate proxy for actual contract expiry, which is a limitation.
6. **US estimated-tax settlement, US500.** On April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15, shifted to the next US nonholiday weekday, at 09:30 New York, short. Cash raised to meet scheduled estimated tax payments may pressure equities. These are simple calendar proxies, not verified filing deadlines for every year.
7. **UK fiscal-year boundary, GBPUSD.** On April 5, shifted to the next UK nonholiday weekday, at 08:00 London, long. Sterling demand from fiscal-year settlement/repatriation may persist through the morning. This date is a fiscal-year proxy rather than an observed payment-flow timestamp.
