# Evidence review (sources checked 20 October 2026)

## Retail outcomes and verified practice

- Barber, Lee, Liu and Odean, *The Cross-Section of Speculator Skill: Evidence from Day Trading* (2014, Journal of Financial Markets; [author manuscript](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/Day%20Traders/The%20Cross-Section%20of%20Speculator%20Skill.pdf)): complete Taiwan exchange records identify a small, persistent profitable tail, less than 1% of day traders. This is evidence that *some* traders have skill, not evidence for SMC.
- Chague, De-Losso and Giovannetti, *Day Trading for a Living?* (2019 working paper; [RePEc record](https://ideas.repec.org/p/spa/wpaper/2019wpecon47.html)): among Brazilian equity-futures newcomers who persisted 300 days, 97% lost money and 0.4% made more than a bank teller. The conditioning on persistence and instrument matters.
- UK Financial Conduct Authority, [2016 CFD account analysis](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/fca-proposes-stricter-rules-contract-difference-products) found 82% of sampled clients lost; [CFD rules](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/cobs22) require broker-specific, rolling 12-month loss warnings inclusive of trading costs. This is account-level evidence, not a universal constant.
- Zarattini and Aziz, [*Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable?*](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.4416622) (2023 preprint) report an ORB simulation on QQQ. Zarattini, Barbon and Aziz, [*A Profitable Day Trading Strategy for the U.S. Equity Market*](https://www.alexandria.unisg.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/3c2989c4-688d-4d78-8a71-f02690990d51/content) (2024 preprint) give replicable 5-minute ORB and stocks-in-play rules. Their base portfolio earned 29% total in 2016–23; their selected top-20-relative-volume portfolio earned 1,637% in their simulation. The improvement is cross-sectional stock selection, which a single index CFD cannot reproduce. The paper describes trading results as a backtest, not audited personal broker statements.
- An independently audited, continuous, strategy-attributed SMC practitioner account was not located in the checked sources. Screenshots, testimonials, and funded-account challenge passes are not verified return series; we do not use them as proof. The Taiwan study's exchange records are a stronger example of verified trader outcomes, although the profitable traders' rules are not identified.
- A useful contrast with marketing claims is the regulated [AQR Managed Futures Strategy Fund SEC prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1444822/000119312526198087/d24166d497k.htm) (AQR, 2026). Its reported Class I returns through 2025 are 11.13% annualized over five years and 3.58% over ten years, including applicable fund fees; its disclosed process seeks price trends across hundreds of commodity, currency, equity and fixed-income instruments with systematic position sizing. These are **fund** results, not a verified intraday SMC trader record and not evidence that the same returns can be earned in CFDs.

## Price levels, breakouts, and stop orders

- Osler, [*Support for Resistance: Technical Analysis and Intraday Exchange Rates*](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/epr/00v06n2/0007osle.pdf) (2000, NY Fed Economic Policy Review): customer-distributed levels from six FX firms forecast some intraday trend interruptions; effectiveness differed by currency and firm. It does not establish a profitable retail trading rule after spread.
- Osler, [*Currency Orders and Exchange Rate Dynamics*](https://doi.org/10.1111/1540-6261.00588) (2003, Journal of Finance): actual FX dealer conditional orders cluster around round numbers, with take profits near and stops beyond. Osler, [*Stop-loss orders and price cascades in currency markets*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jimonfin.2004.12.002) (2005, Journal of International Money and Finance): evidence of faster movement after some round-number crossings. A broker CFD quote has no visibility into those dealer orders.
- Lo, Mamaysky and Wang, [*Foundations of Technical Analysis*](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/19268/Lo-Mamaysky_wang_foundations.pdf) (2000, NBER working paper): algorithmic chart patterns altered conditional return distributions in US stocks. Distributional differences are not automatically executable profits.
- Gao, Han, Li and Zhou, [*Market Intraday Momentum*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2018.05.009) (2018, Journal of Financial Economics): the first half-hour ETF return from the previous close predicts the last half-hour in 1993–2013. The effect is conditional predictive evidence, not an MT5 CFD profit certificate.
- Zarattini, Aziz and Barbon, [*Beat the Market: An Effective Intraday Momentum Strategy for S&P 500 ETF (SPY)*](https://www.wealth-lab.com/api/discussion/download/pdf/9874-ssrn-4824172-pdf) (2024 working paper): 14-day, time-of-day noise bands and band/VWAP exits. Their paper reports attractive SPY backtests; it uses actual ETF volume, which our quote ticks cannot replicate.

## Order flow: what is visible

Real order flow means executed exchange trades with size and inferred/aggressor side, and changing displayed order-book depth. Footprint/delta sums signed executed volume by price; cumulative delta sums it over time. Neither the pending order book nor executed volume is a broker's count of quote updates. Cont, Kukanov and Stoikov, [*The Price Impact of Order Book Events*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402) (2014, Journal of Financial Econometrics) find short-horizon price changes related to best-quote order-flow imbalance. That is microstructure association at very short horizons, not proof that a retail trader can buy the signal, pay costs, and profit at M5–M30.

MT5 CFD bars here contain bid/ask quotes, quote tick count `n`, and counts `up`/`dn` for rising/falling *mid quotes*. They contain no executed trade size, aggressor flag, resting order sizes, or centralized spot-FX book. Calling `(up-dn)/(up+dn)` “real delta” would be false. We test it only as a quote-direction proxy. [CME DataMine](https://www.cmegroup.com/datamine.html) offers historical futures Time & Sales and Market Depth/MBO for ES, NQ, GC and currency futures; a small time-stamped sample aligned to the CFD feed is the sensible purchase before larger spending. It is worth buying only if a prospective matched test demonstrates incremental prediction after fees, latency, and the CFD spread; the current data cannot establish that.

## Assessment before testing

**Known:** retail day trading has a small profitable tail and a large losing majority; conditional orders cluster near some round FX levels; some precisely defined levels and intraday signals have statistical predictive content. **Plausible:** stop-triggered cascades and high-activity breakout selection may matter under specific conditions. **Unproven here:** that FVGs, order blocks, BOS/CHoCH, liquidity grabs, kill zones or CFD tick delta confer independent, durable net profit. An institutional trader's orders are not observable from a candle shape. Spread, data mining, and venue differences can erase a real price-pattern effect.
