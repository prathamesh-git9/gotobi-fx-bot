# Four frozen rules

This reproduces the four-rule RULES.md that was written before the first return calculation. Its contents were temporarily replaced by another concurrent process. The only later correction is the matched random-direction baseline: it now evaluates every real entry, even when counterfactual positions overlap. No signal, stop, target, or parameter changed.

All signals use complete 15-minute bid bars (15 observed M1 rows), left-closed/right-labeled in UTC. Trade XAUUSDm, USTECm, US500m, EURUSDm and GBPUSDm. Signals are allowed Monday-Friday 07:00-18:00 UTC. Entry is next M1 open no more than 2 minutes after completed M15 signal: long ask, short bid. Exit intraday at stop, 2R target, after 4 hours, or 20:00 UTC, first reached. Long exits bid; short exits ask. Same-minute stop and target means stop. A stop gap fills at worse open, target gap receives target only. One open position at a time per rule/symbol. No commission beyond recorded spread, no swap. Risk must be >=3 times entry spread and <=2 times the median range of the previous 20 complete M15 bars. Stop must be on the adverse side of entry. Position size compounds 2% of current equity at initial stop distance, exact fractional CFDs and no margin cap.

1. **PD-break.** Prior UTC calendar day's max high/min low from at least 40 complete M15 bars. Long when M15 closes above prior high after prior M15 close at/below it; short mirror. Signal close must lie in the outer quarter of its M15 range in breakout direction. Enter next M1 open; stop at broken level; target 2R.
2. **PD-retest.** Same prior-day level and breakout condition. Within next four completed M15 bars, long retest touches/breaches broken high and closes above it; short mirror. First such bar signals. Stop at retest bar low/high; target 2R. One pending setup at a time, cancel after four bars.
3. **Channel-break.** Resistance/support = max high/min low of preceding 16 complete M15 bars. Long M15 close above resistance with prior close at/below resistance and current close above close 48 bars earlier; short mirror. Enter next M1 open; stop at broken boundary; target 2R.
4. **BOS-FVG-retest.** Three consecutive complete M15 bars A, B, C have a bullish gap when A high < C low or bearish gap when A low > C high. C also closes beyond max high/min low of the 16 complete bars ending before A, has body >=60% of range and close in outer directional quarter. In next four complete M15 bars, first touch/breach of the gap midpoint followed by close back on displacement side signals entry. Stop at retest bar low/high; target 2R. One pending setup at a time, cancel after four bars.

For every actual rule entry, create a random-direction trade with the same time and absolute stop distance, stop on its adverse side and 2R target. NumPy PCG64 seed is 20261020 + 100 times rule index + symbol index. Counterfactuals may overlap; their compounded equity is diagnostic. Rule returns use one position at a time. No parameter fitting; 2016-10-01 to 2021-12-31 is pre-holdout, and 2022-01-01 to 2026-09-30 is examined once. Report full period (indices start 2019-08), holdout, and 2024-10-01 to 2026-09-30. Four one-sided trade-level t tests of positive holdout average R, Bonferroni p = min(4p,1). Serial/cross-asset dependence makes these p values optimistic.
