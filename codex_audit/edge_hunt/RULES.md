# Frozen research rules (written before return calculations)

All times UTC. Source: supplied Exness Standard bid/ask M1 bars only. Symbols for A/B/D: EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, XAUUSD, USTEC, US500, DE30. A's London rule uses EURUSD, GBPUSD, XAUUSD, DE30. C uses US500, USTEC, XAUUSD, EURUSD. No signals before 2016-10-01 (indices 2019-08-01), after 2026-09-30, or where 200 earlier daily bars are required but unavailable. Consecutive bars must be present for intraday signals/exits. Mid = (bid+ask)/2. All indicators use completed bars and are shifted to avoid looking ahead. Signals formed at bar close, filled at the next bar's observed ask/bid open. Long exits at bid and short at ask. Stop is checked at the unfavorable quote low/high before any time or signal exit, with a worse opening gap filled at that opening price. One position per symbol/rule, no overlapping entries. Missing exit bar: close at the next available open and flag the data gap. No leverage, minimum lot, currency conversion, or margin cap is imposed, so compounded balances are risk illustrations.

R is price P&L divided by the ex-ante stop distance, including observed bid/ask spread. Each rule's standalone balance starts at EUR 1,000, multiplies by `max(0,1+0.02R)` per sequential trade and reports peak-to-trough drawdown. For pooled symbol results, same-time trades are ordered alphabetically and compounded sequentially. Full = 2016-10-01..2026-09-30 (indices from 2019-08-01); recent = 2024-10-01..2026-09-30; holdout = 2022-01-01..2026-09-30. The seeded random-direction control (seed 1729) keeps exactly each rule's entry time, entry reference stop distance, and maximum exit time, replacing its direction by an independently drawn sign. Compare paired trade R. Familywise inference uses one-sided paired t tests and Bonferroni across *all reported candidate rules and symbol-specific B selections*, including negative results. Trade-level t values are descriptive; dependence makes them optimistic.

## A: daily and London rules

Daily bars are UTC-calendar days with at least 60 minutes of quotes; signals use completed daily midpoint OHLC. At most one active trade per rule/symbol. Daily execution uses next UTC day's first quote (not a synthetic midnight price). A daily stop distance is 1x prior 14-day mean true range (ATR) except trend rules at 2x ATR. Time exits use the first quote open of the day after the stated number of held UTC trading days. Daily stop hits use the daily bid low/ask high. Do not add overnight financing to the primary A estimates; E gives a zero-swap rerun and a financing sensitivity. This means the primary daily result is explicitly the swap-free scenario, conditional on account eligibility.

* NR4 and NR7: yesterday's midpoint high-low is strictly less than each of the prior 3 or 6 trading-day ranges. On the next day, the first M1 midpoint high/low crossing yesterday's high/low signals that direction; enter at the next M1 open and exit at the final M1 open of that UTC day, or at stop first. If both thresholds are first crossed in the same M1 bar, skip that day.
* Inside day: yesterday's high < preceding high and low > preceding low. On the next day, trade the first M1 break of yesterday's high/low with the same entry, exit, and ambiguous-bar rule as NR4/7.
* Williams: today's first midpoint open +/- 0.5 x yesterday's high-low defines the two thresholds. Trade the first M1 crossing in its direction, entering next M1 open and exiting at the final M1 open of the same UTC day or at stop first. Skip a same-bar double crossing.
* Turtle Soup: today's high exceeds the previous 20-day high yet closes below it, short tomorrow; mirrored 20-day low for long. Hold 3 days.
* Donchian 20/55: close above previous N-day high or below previous N-day low; enter next day, hold until opposite 10/20-day breakout, respectively, or 60 days maximum. A 2 ATR stop always applies.
* MA trend: 50-day SMA above 200-day SMA signals long and below signals short; enter the following day, hold until the SMA ordering reverses or 60 days. A 2 ATR stop applies.
* Pullback: only US500, USTEC, DE30 and XAUUSD. Close > SMA200 and today's low is the lowest of 3 days; enter long tomorrow, hold 5 days.
* London Asian-range breakout: define range from 00:00-06:59. First completed 15-minute bar from 07:00-09:59 closing outside that range gives direction; enter at next M1 open, exit at first 16:00-or-later quote open the same day or at stop of half Asian range, whichever comes first. Require at least 240 Asian M1 observations and range positive.

## B: hour and weekday seasonality

For each instrument, UTC hour 0..23: buy at first quote open of that hour, exit at the first quote open of the next hour, as available; reverse for negative direction. Weekdays 0..4: first quote open to next UTC day's first quote open. Require an actual next hour/day; disallow holds >90 minutes for hours and >36 hours for weekdays. Each entry has a stop at 1x trailing 14 comparable bar mean true range (at least 10 observations), computed before entry. Scan training 2016-10-01..2021-12-31 (indices 2019-08-01..2021-12-31), selecting the sign with positive training mean and two-sided t-test Benjamini-Hochberg FDR q<0.05 across every instrument-hour and instrument-weekday cell together. A selected cell is traded unchanged on holdout. A separate map of both training and holdout net mean R by hour and weekday will be saved, including unselected cells for transparency. No 2022+ selection.

## C: minute scalps

Use only 2024-10-01..2025-09-30 to design; freeze all variants and evaluate 2025-10-01..2026-09-30 once. Every round trip pays observed spread plus slippage per side of 0.1 pip for EURUSD, or 0.2 basis points of entry price per side for indices and gold. Stop is 1x trailing 60-minute mean absolute M1 mid move times sqrt(hold), floored at 2x (current bid/ask spread + stipulated two-sided slippage). One fixed horizon per idea: (1) fade an M1 return exceeding 2 trailing 60-minute standard deviations, hold 3 minutes; (2) continue M1 return when tick count n exceeds 2x trailing 60-minute mean n, hold 5 minutes; (3) continue a close beyond the previous 30-minute high/low, hold 15 minutes; (4) continue prior M1 sign on the first three minutes after every clock hour, hold 1 minute; (5) continue prior M1 sign on the first five minutes after 09:30 New York local cash open, hold 5 minutes. A 15-minute cooldown follows each trade. All five fixed rules per symbol are counted in multiplicity; no winner tuning.

## D: machine learning

Hourly completed bars. Features at close: midpoint returns over 1, 3, 6, 24 hours; 24-hour return volatility; location in preceding 24-hour range; distance to 24- and 120-hour midpoint SMA; quote count relative to 24-hour mean; UTC hour and weekday. Predict next completed hour direction, separately next 24-hour direction. HistGradientBoostingClassifier(max_iter=100,max_leaf_nodes=15,l2_regularization=1,random_state=1729). Train through each prior calendar year, refit annually, minimum one training year; never train on a target crossing the train/test boundary. The fixed threshold is 0.55, taking long if p>=0.55, short if p<=0.45. Enter at next hour open; horizon 1 or 24 hours. Stop 1x trailing 24-hour true range (hour horizon) or 2x that (day horizon). At most one active position per horizon/symbol. Benchmark is the same-entry seeded random direction. Design years 2016..2021, holdout 2022..2026, with indices available since 2019. No feature/threshold fitting based on holdout.
