# Rules fixed before return calculations

All times below are local to the named market, with daylight saving handled by IANA time zones. Entries and exits use the available one-minute **bar open**; long trades buy at ask and sell at bid, short trades sell at bid and cover at ask. Missing entry/exit bars invalidate a trade (a tolerance of 5 minutes is allowed for the specified clock time). No stop or target is used. A single trade has an unlevered return measured against the entry midprice. Windows never overlap for a given instrument and idea.

Selection data end on 2021-12-31. The evaluation period is 2022-01-01 through 2026-09-30. For USD/JPY Japan ideas, 2006-2015 is a separate untouched historical check with modeled, rather than observed, Exness spreads. A candidate's selection score is its mean net return divided by its standard error; select the largest score, even if negative. The direction of each candidate is fixed below. If an idea spans several instruments, select one instrument using the same score. No tuning after evaluating holdout data.

1. **FOMC:** Long one of US500, US30, USTEC, USD/JPY, EUR/USD, XAU/USD from 14:00 New York on the preceding calendar day until 15 minutes before the scheduled statement. If the preceding calendar day is Saturday/Sunday, skip. This is a long overnight CFD hold. A control uses the same weekdays and clock times one week before each event, excluding an FOMC day/window. The index candidates have data from 2019; the others from 2016.
2. **NYSE pre-holiday:** Long US500 on the final trading weekday before a weekday NYSE holiday, ending at 16:00 New York. Select entry among 12:00, 14:00 and 15:00 New York. A control uses the same weekday and clock times one week earlier, excluding holiday/pre-holiday dates. Any date with no usable US500 quotes is skipped.
3. **Toshin:** Long USD/JPY on the first 1, 2, or 3 Japanese business days of each month, entering at 09:00 and leaving at 10:00 Tokyo. Select the number of days. Control: the same Tokyo window on Japanese business days numbered 6-10 of the same months.
4. **Japan repatriation:** Short USD/JPY on the last 5 or 10 Japanese business days of March and September, 09:00-17:00 Tokyo; select 5 or 10. The proposed reversal is a separate long candidate over the first 5 Japanese business days of April and October, at the same hours. Select the best of these three candidates. Control: the same hours on other Japanese business days in those four months, excluding selected event windows.
5. **Weekend gap:** For each of EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY and XAU/USD, compare the first Sunday bar at or after 22:00 UTC with the last Friday bar at or before 22:00 UTC. Skip if either is absent or more than 4 hours away. Enter 60 minutes after Sunday open, exit 4 or 12 hours after that open. The gap is Sunday mid-open minus Friday mid-close. Select fade or follow, the two exits, and one instrument. Skip gaps smaller than the Friday closing spread. Control: same direction and duration on the following Monday at the Sunday entry's UTC clock time, if quoted.
6. **BoJ decision:** Long USD/JPY on BoJ decision days from 08:00 to 11:00 Tokyo, before the usual decision window; skip days without both quotes. Control: same weekday and clock time one week earlier, excluding BoJ dates.

Selection and inference: One winner is picked per idea. On holdout, test the winner's mean net trade return against zero using a one-sided t statistic in the preselected direction; also test the mean event-minus-control return, matched by event when possible. An idea survives only if both p-values are below 0.05/6 = 0.00833 (Bonferroni for six idea families), has at least 20 holdout trades, and is positive in the 2006-2015 check if it is a Japan idea. Annual pips/points are the sum of bid/ask-executable price changes. This is a deliberately strict screen, not a claim of independent observations. We report the t statistics and p-values, and also yearly results. The historical Japan check is diagnostic, not an extra selection source.

For any survivor, a demonstration compounds EUR 1,000 as USD-equivalent, risking 2% of current equity per trade. Since no stop exists, define the risk unit as 1% of entry midprice: position notional is 2 times equity and trade return on equity is twice the unlevered return. This is a sizing convention, not a bounded loss. Report full available history and the 2025-01-01 through 2026-09-30 slice, max drawdown, number of trades and worst calendar year. Index overnight financing is estimated at 6% annualized on notional by actual hold hours and included in index returns; financing rate is a rough assumption, not broker-verified.
